Paradise on Earth - Kashmir

Kashmir, often called "Paradise on Earth," is a place of breathtaking natural beauty. The snow-capped Himalayan mountains, pristine lakes, lush green meadows, and vibrant gardens make it one of the most beautiful destinations in the world.

From the iconic Dal Lake shikaras to the snow-covered slopes of Gulmarg, from the pine forests of Pahalgam to the glacial beauty of Sonmarg — Kashmir is a dream destination that Musafir Holiday brings to life.

Tour Highlights

  • Dal Lake Shikara Ride in Srinagar
  • Gulmarg Gondola Cable Car Ride
  • Pahalgam - Betaab Valley & Aru Valley
  • Sonmarg - Thajiwas Glacier Trek
  • Mughal Gardens - Shalimar, Nishat, Chashme Shahi
  • Houseboat Stay on Dal Lake
  • Kashmiri Wazwan Cuisine Experience

Sample Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Srinagar

Arrive at Srinagar airport. Transfer to houseboat on Dal Lake. Evening Shikara ride. Overnight in houseboat.

Day 2: Srinagar Local

Visit Mughal Gardens - Shalimar Bagh, Nishat Bagh, Chashme Shahi. Visit Shankaracharya Temple. Evening free for local market. Overnight in Srinagar.

Day 3: Srinagar to Gulmarg

Drive to Gulmarg (2 hrs). Gondola cable car ride. Enjoy snow activities. Evening return to Srinagar or stay in Gulmarg.

Day 4: Srinagar to Pahalgam

Drive to Pahalgam (3 hrs). Visit Betaab Valley, Aru Valley, Chandanwari. Pony ride available. Overnight in Pahalgam.

Day 5: Pahalgam to Sonmarg

Drive to Sonmarg (5 hrs). Visit Thajiwas Glacier. Enjoy the stunning scenery. Return to Srinagar. Overnight stay.

Day 6: Departure

After breakfast, transfer to Srinagar airport. Tour ends with paradise memories!
