Sun, Sand & Fun - Goa

Goa is India's ultimate beach destination, famous for its golden beaches, Portuguese architecture, vibrant nightlife, and delicious seafood. Whether you're looking for a relaxing beach holiday or an adventure-filled trip, Goa has it all.

Musafir Holiday offers the best Goa packages for couples, families, and friend groups. Explore North Goa's beaches and nightlife or South Goa's serene beauty and heritage churches.

Tour Highlights

  • Baga, Calangute & Anjuna Beaches
  • Water Sports - Parasailing, Jet Ski, Banana Ride
  • Basilica of Bom Jesus & Se Cathedral
  • Dudhsagar Waterfalls Trip
  • Cruise & Casino Experience
  • Spice Plantation Visit
  • Old Goa Heritage Walk

Sample Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Goa

Arrive at Goa airport. Transfer to hotel. Evening leisure at Calangute Beach. Overnight in North Goa.

Day 2: North Goa Tour

Visit Fort Aguada, Baga Beach, Anjuna Beach, Chapora Fort. Water sports & beach activities. Evening nightlife at Tito's Lane.

Day 3: South Goa Tour

Visit Old Goa churches, Mangueshi Temple, Palolem Beach, Colva Beach. Evening sunset cruise on Mandovi River.

Day 4: Dudhsagar & Spice Plantation

Full day excursion to Dudhsagar Waterfalls & spice plantation. Enjoy traditional Goan lunch. Return by evening.

Day 5: Departure

Leisure morning. Last-minute shopping at Mapusa Market. Transfer to airport. Tour ends!
