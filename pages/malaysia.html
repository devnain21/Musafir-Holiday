Truly Asia - Malaysia

Malaysia is a vibrant Southeast Asian destination offering a diverse mix of cultures, cuisines, and landscapes. From the iconic Petronas Twin Towers in Kuala Lumpur to the pristine beaches of Langkawi, Malaysia has something for every traveler.

Musafir Holiday offers comprehensive Malaysia tour packages covering Kuala Lumpur, Genting Highlands, Langkawi, Penang, and more — ideal for families, couples, and adventure seekers.

Tour Highlights

  • Petronas Twin Towers & KLCC
  • Genting Highlands Theme Park
  • Langkawi Island Tour & Cable Car
  • Batu Caves Temple
  • Penang Georgetown Heritage Walk
  • Aquaria KLCC
  • Malaysian Street Food Experience

Sample Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in KL

Arrive at KLIA. Transfer to hotel. Evening visit Petronas Towers & KLCC Park. Overnight in KL.

Day 2: KL City Tour

Visit Batu Caves, National Mosque, Independence Square, KL Tower. Afternoon at Bukit Bintang for shopping.

Day 3: Genting Highlands

Day trip to Genting Highlands by cable car. Theme park, casino & SkyAvenue Mall. Return to KL.

Day 4: KL to Langkawi

Fly to Langkawi. Island hopping tour - Pregnant Maiden Island, Singa Besar, Wet Rice Island. Overnight in Langkawi.

Day 5: Langkawi Sightseeing

SkyBridge & Cable Car, Underwater World, Kilim Geoforest Park. Evening Pantai Cenang beach. Overnight.

Day 6: Departure

Free morning for duty-free shopping. Transfer to Langkawi airport. Tour ends!
