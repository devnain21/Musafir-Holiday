Tropical Paradise - Maldives

The Maldives is the ultimate tropical paradise, famous for its crystal-clear turquoise waters, overwater villas, white sandy beaches, and vibrant marine life. It's the perfect destination for honeymooners, couples, and anyone seeking a luxurious island escape.

Musafir Holiday offers handpicked Maldives packages with stays at stunning resorts, water sports, diving experiences, and romantic sunset cruises — creating memories that last a lifetime.

Tour Highlights

  • Overwater Villa Stay
  • Snorkeling with Marine Life
  • Sunset Dolphin Cruise
  • Scuba Diving Experience
  • Private Beach Dining
  • Island Hopping Tour
  • Spa & Wellness Treatments

Sample Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Malé

Arrive at Velana International Airport, Malé. Speedboat/seaplane transfer to resort. Welcome drink & resort orientation. Relax at beach. Overnight at resort.

Day 2: Water Activities

Morning snorkeling trip to coral reefs. Afternoon water sports - kayaking, paddleboarding. Evening sunset dolphin cruise. Romantic beach dinner.

Day 3: Island Exploration

Island hopping excursion to local islands. Experience Maldivian culture & cuisine. Afternoon spa treatment. Evening stargazing from overwater deck.

Day 4: Leisure Day

Free day to enjoy resort amenities. Optional scuba diving, fishing trip, or sandbank picnic. Farewell dinner at underwater/overwater restaurant.

Day 5: Departure

Breakfast at resort. Transfer to Malé airport. Tour ends with paradise memories!
