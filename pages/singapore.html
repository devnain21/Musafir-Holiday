The Lion City - Singapore

Singapore is a dazzling city-state that perfectly blends modernity with tradition. From the iconic Marina Bay Sands to the thrilling rides at Universal Studios, from the lush Gardens by the Bay to the bustling hawker centres, Singapore is a complete holiday destination.

Musafir Holiday offers carefully planned Singapore packages perfect for families, couples, and honeymooners looking for a mix of entertainment, shopping, and cultural experiences.

Tour Highlights

  • Marina Bay Sands SkyPark
  • Sentosa Island & Universal Studios
  • Gardens by the Bay & Cloud Forest
  • Singapore Zoo & Night Safari
  • Orchard Road Shopping
  • Merlion Park & Clarke Quay
  • S.E.A. Aquarium

Sample Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Singapore

Arrive at Changi Airport. Transfer to hotel. Evening visit Marina Bay Sands & Spectra Light Show. Overnight in Singapore.

Day 2: City Tour

Visit Merlion Park, Chinatown, Little India, Gardens by the Bay (OCBC Skyway & Cloud Forest). Evening at Clarke Quay.

Day 3: Sentosa Island

Full day at Sentosa - Universal Studios, S.E.A. Aquarium, Adventure Cove. Evening Wings of Time show.

Day 4: Zoo & Shopping

Morning Singapore Zoo visit. Afternoon Orchard Road shopping. Evening Night Safari experience.

Day 5: Departure

Free morning for last-minute shopping at Mustafa Centre. Transfer to Changi Airport. Tour ends!
