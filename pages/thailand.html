Land of Smiles - Thailand

Thailand is one of Southeast Asia's most popular destinations, known for its stunning beaches, ornate temples, delicious street food, and vibrant nightlife. From the bustling capital Bangkok to the tropical islands of Phuket, Thailand offers an unforgettable experience.

Musafir Holiday crafts the perfect Thailand tour packages covering Bangkok, Pattaya, Phuket, Krabi, and Chiang Mai — ideal for couples, families, and friends.

Tour Highlights

  • Grand Palace & Wat Pho in Bangkok
  • Pattaya Beach & Coral Island
  • Phi Phi Islands Tour from Phuket
  • Floating Market Experience
  • Thai Massage & Spa Treatment
  • Alcazar Cabaret Show
  • Safari World & Marine Park

Sample Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Bangkok

Arrive at Suvarnabhumi Airport. Transfer to hotel. Evening visit Asiatique The Riverfront for dinner cruise. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 2: Bangkok City Tour

Visit Grand Palace, Wat Pho (Reclining Buddha), Wat Arun. Afternoon at Chatuchak Market. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 3: Bangkok to Pattaya

Drive to Pattaya (2 hrs). Visit Coral Island with water sports. Evening Walking Street. Overnight in Pattaya.

Day 4: Pattaya Sightseeing

Visit Nong Nooch Garden, Art in Paradise, Floating Market. Evening Alcazar Show. Overnight in Pattaya.

Day 5: Pattaya to Bangkok

Free morning for shopping at Pattaya. Drive back to Bangkok. Evening MBK/Platinum Mall shopping. Overnight in Bangkok.

Day 6: Departure

Free morning. Transfer to Bangkok airport. Tour ends with amazing Thai memories!
