Explore the Royal Land of Rajasthan

Rajasthan, the Land of Kings, is India's most colourful and vibrant state. From the pink city of Jaipur to the golden sands of Jaisalmer, from the blue city of Jodhpur to the lake city of Udaipur — every corner tells a story of valor, romance and grandeur.

Musafir Holiday offers carefully crafted Rajasthan tour packages that cover the best heritage sites, desert experiences, royal palaces, and cultural attractions. Whether you're a history buff, adventure seeker, or honeymooner, Rajasthan has something magical for everyone.

Tour Highlights

  • Amer Fort & Hawa Mahal in Jaipur
  • Mehrangarh Fort & Blue City Walk in Jodhpur
  • Lake Palace & City Palace in Udaipur
  • Camel Safari in Jaisalmer Sand Dunes
  • Pushkar Brahma Temple & Ghats
  • Traditional Rajasthani Folk Dance & Dinner
  • Ranthambore National Park Tiger Safari

Sample Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Jaipur

Welcome at Jaipur airport/station. Transfer to hotel. Evening visit to Birla Mandir & local market. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 2: Jaipur Sightseeing

Visit Amer Fort (elephant ride), Jal Mahal, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar. Evening at Chokhi Dhani village resort.

Day 3: Jaipur to Jodhpur

Drive to Jodhpur via Ajmer & Pushkar. Visit Brahma Temple and Pushkar Lake. Arrive Jodhpur. Overnight stay.

Day 4: Jodhpur to Jaisalmer

Morning visit Mehrangarh Fort, Jaswant Thada. Drive to Jaisalmer. Evening at Sam Sand Dunes with camel safari & folk performance.

Day 5: Jaisalmer to Udaipur

Visit Jaisalmer Fort, Patwon Ki Haveli, Gadisar Lake. Drive to Udaipur via Ranakpur Jain Temple.

Day 6: Udaipur Sightseeing

Visit City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Saheliyon ki Bari, Fateh Sagar Lake. Evening boat ride on Lake Pichola.

Day 7: Departure

After breakfast, transfer to Udaipur airport/station for onward journey. Tour ends with beautiful memories!
