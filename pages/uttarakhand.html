Dev Bhoomi - Uttarakhand

Uttarakhand, known as Dev Bhoomi (Land of the Gods), is a spiritual and natural paradise in the Indian Himalayas. From holy cities like Haridwar and Rishikesh to the serene hill stations of Mussoorie and Nainital, Uttarakhand offers a perfect blend of spirituality, nature, and adventure.

Musafir Holiday brings you the best Uttarakhand packages covering pilgrimage, adventure, and leisure — making it perfect for families, couples, and spiritual seekers.

Tour Highlights

  • Ganga Aarti at Haridwar Har Ki Pauri
  • River Rafting in Rishikesh
  • Mussoorie - Queen of Hills
  • Nainital Lake Boating
  • Jim Corbett National Park Safari
  • Auli Skiing & Cable Car
  • Char Dham Yatra (Seasonal)

Sample Itinerary

Day 1: Delhi to Haridwar

Drive from Delhi to Haridwar (5 hrs). Visit Har Ki Pauri. Attend the mesmerizing Ganga Aarti. Overnight in Haridwar.

Day 2: Haridwar to Rishikesh

Drive to Rishikesh (1 hr). Visit Lakshman Jhula, Ram Jhula, Beatles Ashram. Optional river rafting. Overnight in Rishikesh.

Day 3: Rishikesh to Mussoorie

Drive to Mussoorie (5 hrs). Visit Kempty Falls, Company Garden. Evening stroll on Mall Road. Overnight in Mussoorie.

Day 4: Mussoorie Sightseeing

Visit Gun Hill by cable car, Lal Tibba, Cloud's End. Enjoy panoramic Himalayan views. Overnight in Mussoorie.

Day 5: Mussoorie to Nainital

Drive to Nainital (7 hrs). Enjoy boating on Naini Lake. Visit Naina Devi Temple. Overnight in Nainital.

Day 6: Departure

Visit Mall Road for shopping. Drive back to Delhi. Tour ends with divine memories!
