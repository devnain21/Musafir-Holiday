Mountain Paradise - Himachal Pradesh

Himachal Pradesh is a mountainous state in northern India offering breathtaking landscapes, adventure activities, and serene hill stations. From the colonial charm of Shimla to the adventure capital Manali, every destination is unique and mesmerizing.

Musafir Holiday brings you the best Himachal tour packages covering Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala, Dalhousie, Kullu, and more. Perfect for honeymooners, families, and adventure seekers.

Tour Highlights

  • Shimla Mall Road & Ridge
  • Manali - Solang Valley Adventure Sports
  • Rohtang Pass / Atal Tunnel
  • Kullu River Rafting
  • Dharamshala & McLeod Ganj
  • Hadimba Temple & Old Manali
  • Kufri Snow Activities

Sample Itinerary

Day 1: Delhi to Shimla

Departure from Delhi. Drive to Shimla (8 hrs). Evening free for Mall Road walk. Overnight in Shimla.

Day 2: Shimla Sightseeing

Visit Kufri, Jakhu Temple, Christ Church, The Ridge. Enjoy snow activities in Kufri (seasonal). Overnight in Shimla.

Day 3: Shimla to Manali

Drive to Manali via Kullu (7 hrs). En route visit Kullu Shawl factories. Overnight in Manali.

Day 4: Manali Sightseeing

Visit Hadimba Temple, Manu Temple, Vashisht Hot Springs, Old Manali, Club House. Overnight in Manali.

Day 5: Solang Valley / Rohtang

Excursion to Solang Valley or Rohtang Pass (subject to permit). Adventure sports - paragliding, zorbing. Overnight in Manali.

Day 6: Departure

After breakfast, drive back to Delhi. Tour ends with beautiful mountain memories!
