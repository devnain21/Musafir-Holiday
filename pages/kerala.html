God's Own Country - Kerala

Kerala, known as God's Own Country, is a tropical paradise in South India. With its serene backwaters, lush tea plantations, pristine beaches, and rich cultural heritage, Kerala offers an experience unlike any other destination.

From the misty hills of Munnar to the tranquil houseboats of Alleppey, from the wildlife sanctuary of Periyar to the beaches of Kovalam — Musafir Holiday brings you the best of Kerala.

Tour Highlights

  • Alleppey Backwater Houseboat Stay
  • Munnar Tea Garden Visit
  • Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary
  • Kovalam Beach Getaway
  • Kathakali Dance Performance
  • Cochin Fort Kochi Heritage Walk
  • Spice Plantation Tour

Sample Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Cochin

Arrive at Cochin airport. Visit Fort Kochi, Chinese Fishing Nets, St. Francis Church, and Jewish Synagogue. Evening Kathakali show. Overnight in Cochin.

Day 2: Cochin to Munnar

Drive to Munnar (4 hrs). En route visit Cheeyappara & Valara Waterfalls. Evening visit tea gardens. Overnight in Munnar.

Day 3: Munnar Sightseeing

Visit Eravikulam National Park, Mattupetty Dam, Tea Museum, Echo Point. Overnight in Munnar.

Day 4: Munnar to Thekkady

Drive to Thekkady (3 hrs). Visit spice plantations. Evening Periyar Lake boating. Overnight in Thekkady.

Day 5: Thekkady to Alleppey

Drive to Alleppey (4 hrs). Board traditional houseboat. Cruise through serene backwaters. Overnight on houseboat.

Day 6: Alleppey to Kovalam

Disembark houseboat. Drive to Kovalam beach (4 hrs). Evening leisure at beach. Overnight in Kovalam.

Day 7: Departure

Visit Trivandrum Padmanabhaswamy Temple. Transfer to Trivandrum airport. Tour ends!
