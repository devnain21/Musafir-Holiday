City of Gold - Dubai

Dubai is a city of superlatives — the tallest building, the largest shopping mall, the most luxurious hotels. This dazzling emirate in the UAE combines ultramodern architecture with traditional Arabian culture, desert adventures, and world-class shopping.

Musafir Holiday offers the best Dubai packages covering city tours, desert safari, Abu Dhabi excursion, Dhow cruise, theme parks, and unforgettable experiences for families and couples.

Tour Highlights

  • Burj Khalifa - At the Top
  • Desert Safari with BBQ Dinner
  • Dubai Mall & Aquarium
  • Dhow Cruise Marina
  • Abu Dhabi Day Tour
  • Palm Jumeirah & Atlantis
  • Gold Souk & Spice Market

Sample Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Dubai

Arrive at Dubai International Airport. Transfer to hotel. Evening Dhow Cruise with dinner on Dubai Marina. Overnight in Dubai.

Day 2: Dubai City Tour

Half-day city tour - Jumeirah Mosque, Dubai Creek, Gold & Spice Souk, Dubai Museum. Afternoon at Dubai Mall. Evening Burj Khalifa visit & Dubai Fountain show.

Day 3: Desert Safari

Free morning for shopping. Afternoon desert safari with dune bashing, camel riding, sandboarding, BBQ dinner with belly dance show.

Day 4: Abu Dhabi Tour

Full day Abu Dhabi excursion. Visit Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Yas Island, Ferrari World (optional), Louvre Abu Dhabi. Return to Dubai.

Day 5: Leisure & Departure

Visit Atlantis Aquaventure or Global Village (seasonal). Last-minute shopping. Transfer to airport. Tour ends!
